NavList: a new `NavList` / `NavList.Item` / `NavList.Group` for side navigation and in-page filter rails. Rows are native links (`aria-current="page"`, `asChild` for `next/link`) or buttons (`aria-current="true"`), with `leadingVisual`, `trailingVisual`, `trailingAction` and `isDisabled`; the current row is ink and bold on `bg.emphasized`. `orientation` takes a per-breakpoint object so a rail can lie down into a sideways-scrolling strip below `md`. Also exports `focusRingInset` / `FOCUS_RING_INSET_SHADOW`, the inset counterpart of `focusRing` for rows in a dense or scrolling list. `isCollapsed` gives the icon-only rail (label as accessible name and tooltip); `tooltip` adds a described-by tooltip, and a truncated label shows itself in full on hover/focus; `target="_blank"` links announce "(새 창 열림)"; the current row carries `data-current` and a horizontal strip scrolls it into view; `trailingActionVisibility="hover"` reveals the trailing action on hover or focus without taking it out of the tab order; `NavList.Group isCollapsible` is a disclosure with controlled or uncontrolled state; `NavList.Skeleton` stands in while entries load.
