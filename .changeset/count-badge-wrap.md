New `CountBadgeAnchor` puts a `CountBadge` on the top-end corner of the icon
or avatar it wraps (`children`). `ringColor` draws a 2px ring outside the badge
in the colour behind it, and `invisible` hides the badge but keeps the icon.
`CountBadge` itself is unchanged.
